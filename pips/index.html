How to play

Place every domino on the board so that each coloured region meets its rule.

  • 7 the pips in the region add up to 7
  • <5 / >5 the total is under / over 5
  • = every pip count in the region is the same
  • ≠ every pip count in the region is different
  • Grey squares have no rule.

Tap a domino to pick it up, tap it again (or press R) to rotate, then tap a square to drop it. Tap a placed domino to take it back.
