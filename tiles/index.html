How to play

  • Each tile is built from up to three layers: a background colour, a shape, and a small symbol.
  • Select two tiles that share a layer. Every matching layer disappears from both.
  • The second tile stays selected. Match it with another tile to keep your combo going.
  • Picking a tile with nothing in common breaks the combo. Clear the whole board to win!
